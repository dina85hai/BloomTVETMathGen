# Research Evidence Policy

The research registry is used to constrain misconception diagnostics and avoid unsupported claims.

## A
Direct Malaysian/TVET empirical evidence for the exact misconception/error family in a closely matched population.

## B
Direct empirical evidence for the misconception/error family, but not necessarily in Malaysian TVET learners.

## C
Related empirical support for the broader conceptual difficulty; exact generated diagnostic item requires local validation.

## D
Structure-grounded/research-informed diagnostic hypothesis; do not describe as proven.

## Claim rule

Never claim local prevalence, effect size or proof unless the source record explicitly establishes it. Never invent author names, citations, standards, sample sizes or percentages.

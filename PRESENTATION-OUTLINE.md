# BloomTVET MathGen Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title

**BloomTVET MathGen**

AI assessment standardization and learning support platform for TVET mathematics.

Suggested subtitle: Standardize Bloom-based assessment, then support student understanding through BloomCoach AI.

## Slide 2: Core Problem

- TVET lecturers need to prepare questions with consistent Bloom level and difficulty.
- Manual question preparation takes time and can vary between lecturers.
- Question quality depends on clear alignment to topic, Bloom taxonomy, difficulty and marking scheme.
- Students also need support to understand why they make mistakes after receiving questions.
- Most AI tools are too general and are not designed around TVET assessment standards.

## Slide 3: Main Aim

The primary aim is to help lecturers standardize TVET mathematics assessment.

The app focuses on:

- Bloom C1 to C4 question design
- Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty calibration
- Subjective constructed-response format
- TVET workplace context
- Consistent 10-mark marking schemes
- Misconception diagnostics
- Evidence-informed assessment design

## Slide 4: Proposed Solution

BloomTVET MathGen combines two connected modules:

1. **Assessment Standardization Module**
   - For lecturers
   - Generates structured Bloom-aligned questions
   - Produces marking schemes, visuals and evidence labels

2. **BloomCoach AI Learning Support Module**
   - For students and lecturers
   - Explains questions, gives hints, creates stories, suggests visuals and reviews assessment quality

## Slide 5: User Roles

**Lecturer Role**

- Generate standardized assessment questions
- Review Bloom level and difficulty
- Improve question wording
- Save, export and validate questions
- Collect teacher feedback and student results

**Student Role**

- Understand question meaning
- Receive hints and step-by-step guidance
- View daily-life or TVET workplace explanations
- Check answers and diagnose mistakes

## Slide 6: Key Features

- 26 mathematics subtopics
- 14 TVET clusters
- Bloom C1 to C4
- Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty
- Built-in deterministic question bank
- Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic live AI support
- English, Bahasa Melayu and bilingual output
- Visual diagrams for questions
- 10-mark marking schemes
- Misconception diagnostics
- Research evidence labels
- PDF, SVG, JSON, Google Classroom and Canvas QTI exports

## Slide 7: BloomCoach AI

BloomCoach AI is the live chatbot inside the platform.

It supports:

- Explain Mode
- Hint Mode
- Step-by-Step Mode
- Visual Mode
- Story Mode
- Misconception Mode
- Lecturer Review Mode
- Challenge Mode

## Slide 8: BloomCoach AI For Students

Student support includes:

- Simplifying difficult question wording
- Explaining what the question is asking
- Giving progressive hints without immediately revealing the answer
- Guiding students step by step
- Converting abstract math into daily-life or TVET workplace stories
- Suggesting diagrams, flowcharts, tables or visual models
- Checking student attempts
- Identifying possible misconceptions

## Slide 9: BloomCoach AI For Lecturers

Lecturer support includes:

- Reviewing Bloom alignment
- Reviewing difficulty alignment
- Checking 10-mark marking scheme quality
- Suggesting clearer question wording
- Suggesting improved TVET context
- Suggesting alternative question versions
- Supporting moderation and standardization

## Slide 10: User Workflow

1. Lecturer selects topic, Bloom level, difficulty, TVET field and language.
2. App generates a subjective assessment question.
3. Lecturer reviews model answer, marking scheme, visual and misconception diagnostics.
4. Lecturer opens BloomCoach AI for review or student support.
5. Student uses BloomCoach AI to understand, receive hints, visualize and check answers.
6. Lecturer records feedback and student results for analytics.

## Slide 13: Innovation

The innovation is the full assessment-to-learning loop:

- Lecturer standardizes assessment.
- Student receives guided learning support.
- Misconceptions are diagnosed.
- Teacher feedback and student results feed the dashboard.

This makes BloomTVET MathGen more than a question generator. It becomes an assessment and learning support platform.

## Slide 14: Impact

- Reduces lecturer preparation time.
- Improves consistency of question difficulty.
- Supports Bloom-based assessment.
- Helps students understand mathematical problems.
- Promotes bilingual learning.
- Connects mathematics to real TVET workplace situations.
- Supports evidence-informed teaching and assessment.
